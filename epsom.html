How to Get the Most Out of Your Soak

Epsom salt isn't complicated, but a few simple things make a real difference in how effective it actually is.

💧

Warm, Not Hot

Scalding water can be hard on your skin. Warm water opens your pores just enough for the magnesium to absorb properly without stripping your skin.

⏱️

20 Minutes Minimum

Your body needs at least 20 minutes to start absorbing the magnesium. Set a timer, put your phone in the other room, and actually let yourself rest.

📏

Use Enough Salt

Most soaks recommend 1–2 cups for a full bath. Don't skimp — you need a real concentration of magnesium for it to be effective, not just a sprinkle.

🌿

Add Essential Oils

Lavender for calm, eucalyptus for respiratory support, peppermint for muscle relief. A few drops of the right oil makes your soak work harder for you.
