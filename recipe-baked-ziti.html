Homemade Baked Ziti

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Baked ziti is my go-to when I need to feed a lot of people without spending a lot of money. It's pasta, meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted mozzarella all baked together until bubbly and golden on top. It's one of those dishes that everybody loves and nobody ever turns down seconds.

It also reheats perfectly, which means it's great for meal prep. I make it on Sunday and we eat it all week. The flavors actually get better the next day.

What You Need — Serves 6–8

  • 1 lb ziti or penne pasta
  • 1 lb ground beef or Italian sausage
  • 3 cups tomato pasta sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella, divided
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper
  • Fresh basil to finish

How to Make It

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Cook pasta in well-salted water until 2 minutes shy of al dente — it finishes cooking in the oven. Drain and set aside.
  2. Brown the ground beef or sausage in a large skillet, breaking it up. Drain fat. Add pasta sauce, Italian seasoning, and garlic powder. Simmer 5 minutes.
  3. In a bowl, mix ricotta, egg, salt, and pepper until smooth. Stir in 1 cup of the mozzarella.
  4. In a large bowl, toss the cooked pasta with the meat sauce until well coated.
  5. Spread half the pasta in a 9x13 baking dish. Dollop the ricotta mixture over the top. Add the remaining pasta.
  6. Sprinkle the remaining 1 cup mozzarella and all the Parmesan over the top.
  7. Cover with foil and bake 30 minutes. Remove foil and bake 15 more minutes until the cheese is golden and the edges are bubbling.
  8. Rest 10 minutes before serving. Top with fresh basil.

Harley's tip: Don't skip the two-minute undercook on the pasta. If you cook it all the way before baking, it comes out mushy. Pull it slightly early and it finishes perfectly in the oven.
