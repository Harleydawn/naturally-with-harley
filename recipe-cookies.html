My Go-To Cookies

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

I've made this cookie recipe so many times I don't even need to look at it anymore. Crispy golden edges, soft chewy centers, real butter, real chocolate — this is the one. I've brought these to school events, family gatherings, and random Tuesday afternoons at home, and they disappear every single time.

No fancy tricks, no chilling the dough overnight. Just a great cookie you can have on a plate in 30 minutes.

What You Need — Makes about 4 dozen cookies

  • 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) butter, softened to room temperature
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

How to Make It

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking soda, and salt together in a bowl. Set aside.
  3. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter with both sugars until light and creamy — about 2 minutes.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla, and beat until fluffy.
  5. Gradually stir in the flour mixture until just combined. Don't overmix.
  6. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  7. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto ungreased baking sheets, spacing them 2 inches apart.
  8. Bake 9–11 minutes. The edges should be golden but the centers will still look slightly underdone — that's perfect. They firm up as they cool.
  9. Let sit on the baking sheet for 2 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.

Harley's tip: Pull them out when they still look a little underdone in the middle. I know it feels wrong but trust the process — they'll be perfectly soft once they cool. Overbaked cookies are the only bad cookie.

Room temperature butter is non-negotiable here. Cold butter won't cream properly and your cookies will spread too much. Set it out an hour before you start and you'll get perfect results every time.
