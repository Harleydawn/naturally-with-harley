Chimichurri Sauce

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Chimichurri is an Argentinian herb sauce made with fresh parsley, garlic, olive oil, and red wine vinegar. It's bright, herby, and garlicky — and it makes grilled chicken, steak, or fish taste like it came from a restaurant. I started making it because I wanted a clean alternative to bottled sauces, and now it's a weekly staple.

No cooking required. Just chop and mix.

What You Need — Makes about 1 cup

  • 1 cup fresh flat-leaf parsley, tightly packed
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro (optional)
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ cup red wine vinegar
  • ½ cup olive oil
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

How to Make It

  1. Finely chop the parsley and cilantro by hand — don't use a food processor or it turns into a paste. You want a chunky, rustic texture.
  2. Combine the chopped herbs, garlic, red wine vinegar, red pepper flakes, salt, pepper, and oregano in a bowl.
  3. Stir in the olive oil until everything is combined.
  4. Taste and adjust — more vinegar for brightness, more pepper flakes for heat, more salt to bring it together.
  5. Let sit for at least 15 minutes before serving so the flavors meld. It gets better the longer it sits.

Harley's tip: This keeps in the fridge for up to a week. The olive oil will solidify — just let it come to room temperature for a few minutes before serving and stir it back together. It's even better on day 2.
