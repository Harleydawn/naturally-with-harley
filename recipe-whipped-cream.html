Homemade Whipped Cream

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Two ingredients. Two minutes. And it tastes like nothing you've ever had from a can. Light, fluffy, lightly sweet, and made with real heavy cream — this is whipped cream the way it's supposed to be.

I put this on everything: pies, pancakes, hot cocoa, fresh berries, brownies, cinnamon rolls. Once you make it yourself, the canned stuff just doesn't compare.

What You Need — Makes about 2 cups

  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream (cold)
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • ½ tsp pure vanilla extract

How to Make It

  1. Make sure your cream is very cold — this is the most important step. Put your bowl and beaters in the freezer for 10 minutes if you can.
  2. Pour the cold cream into the chilled bowl.
  3. Beat on medium-high speed until the cream starts to thicken, about 1 minute.
  4. Add the powdered sugar and vanilla. Continue beating until soft or stiff peaks form, depending on how you like it.
  5. Use immediately or refrigerate for up to 24 hours.

Harley's tip: Soft peaks means it's light and cloud-like — great for topping hot drinks or fruit. Stiff peaks means it holds its shape — better for piping or dolloping on pie. Watch it closely once it thickens; a few seconds too long and it starts turning into butter.

Want to make it ahead? Whip to stiff peaks, store in a covered container in the fridge, and give it a quick stir before serving. It holds up well for most of a day.
