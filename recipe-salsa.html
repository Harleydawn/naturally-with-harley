Homemade Fresh Salsa

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Fresh tomatoes, cilantro, lime, jalapeño, and garlic — made in minutes and so much better than anything from a jar. This is taco night in our house every single week. I make a big batch and it disappears before dinner is even on the table.

No cooking required. Just chop, mix, and taste. The hardest part is not eating the whole bowl before the tacos are ready.

What You Need — Makes about 2 cups

  • 4 roma tomatoes, diced
  • ½ white onion, finely diced
  • 1–2 jalapeños, seeded and minced (use more for heat)
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Juice of 1–2 limes
  • ½ tsp salt (more to taste)
  • ¼ tsp cumin (optional)

How to Make It

  1. Dice the tomatoes and place in a bowl. If they're very watery, let them drain in a strainer for a few minutes first.
  2. Add the diced onion, jalapeño, and garlic.
  3. Stir in the cilantro, lime juice, and salt.
  4. Add cumin if using.
  5. Taste and adjust — more lime for brightness, more salt to bring out the flavor, more jalapeño for heat.
  6. Let it sit for at least 15 minutes before serving so the flavors come together.

Harley's tip: Roma tomatoes work best because they're less watery than regular tomatoes. If all you have are regular tomatoes, scoop out the seeds and watery center before dicing so your salsa doesn't get soupy. Also — this gets even better overnight in the fridge.
