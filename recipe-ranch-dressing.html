Homemade Ranch Dressing

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Creamy, herby, garlicky, and made with real ingredients — not a powder packet. My family puts this on everything and I love that I know exactly what's in it. No weird additives, no ingredients I can't pronounce. Just good stuff.

Takes five minutes to make and tastes so much better than anything from a bottle. This is one of those recipes that once you make it yourself, you just never go back.

What You Need — Makes about 1½ cups

  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 2–3 tbsp buttermilk (or regular milk)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tbsp fresh chives, finely chopped (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 1 tbsp fresh dill, finely chopped (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste

How to Make It

  1. Whisk together the mayonnaise and sour cream in a bowl until smooth.
  2. Add the buttermilk one tablespoon at a time until you reach your desired consistency — thicker for dip, thinner for dressing.
  3. Stir in the garlic powder, onion powder, chives, dill, parsley, and lemon juice.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving — the flavor gets much better as it sits.
  6. Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 1 week.

Harley's tip: Fresh herbs make a noticeable difference here. If you have them, use them. But dried herbs work great too — just use about a third of the amount since dried herbs are more concentrated. Also, the longer this sits in the fridge, the better it gets.
