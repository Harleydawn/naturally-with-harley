Homemade Hamburger Buns

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

A really good burger deserves a really good bun — and these are it. Soft, fluffy, golden on top, and they actually hold together when you load them up. The egg in the dough gives them a slight richness that makes them taste like something from a bakery, not a grocery bag.

Burger night changed completely when I started making these. My family noticed immediately.

What You Need — Makes 8 buns

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2¼ tsp active dry yeast (one packet)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp butter, softened
  • 1 large egg
  • ¾ cup warm milk
  • ¼ cup warm water
  • For the top: 1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp water, sesame seeds (optional)

How to Make It

  1. Dissolve the yeast and a pinch of sugar in the warm water. Let sit 5–10 minutes until foamy.
  2. In a large bowl, combine flour, salt, and sugar. Add the yeast mixture, warm milk, softened butter, and egg. Mix until a dough forms.
  3. Knead on a floured surface for 8–10 minutes until the dough is smooth and elastic.
  4. Place in an oiled bowl, cover, and let rise 1 hour until doubled.
  5. Punch down the dough and divide into 8 equal pieces. Roll each into a smooth ball and then gently flatten into a disc about ¾ inch thick.
  6. Place on a parchment-lined baking sheet with room to spread. Cover and let rise 30 minutes.
  7. Preheat oven to 375°F. Brush the tops with egg wash and sprinkle sesame seeds if you like.
  8. Bake 15–18 minutes until deep golden brown on top.
  9. Cool on a wire rack before slicing.

Harley's tip: Don't skip the egg wash — it's what gives you that beautiful shiny golden top. And let them cool fully before slicing or the inside will be gummy.

These freeze perfectly. Make a double batch and freeze half. Thaw on the counter overnight and they're just as good as fresh. Best thing I ever did for busy weeknight dinners.
