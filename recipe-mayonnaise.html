Homemade Mayonnaise

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Homemade mayonnaise is one of the most satisfying things you can make from scratch. It comes together in under two minutes with an immersion blender, and the result is rich, creamy, and nothing like the shelf-stable stuff in jars. Real mayo has four ingredients: egg, oil, lemon, and mustard. That's it.

Once you try this you'll understand why people talk about it. The flavor and texture are on a completely different level.

What You Need — Makes about 1 cup

  • 1 large egg, room temperature
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice (or white wine vinegar)
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 cup neutral oil (like avocado oil or light olive oil)

How to Make It

  1. Add the egg, lemon juice, mustard, and salt to a tall, narrow jar or the cup that came with your immersion blender.
  2. Pour the oil on top. Do not stir.
  3. Place the immersion blender at the bottom of the jar, touching the egg yolk.
  4. Turn on the blender and hold it still at the bottom for about 10 seconds until you see white creamy mayo forming.
  5. Slowly lift the blender upward through the mixture, taking about 10–15 more seconds until everything is fully emulsified and creamy.
  6. Taste and adjust with more salt or lemon. Transfer to a jar and refrigerate for up to 1 week.

Harley's tip: The key is using room temperature eggs and not moving the blender too fast. Cold eggs can cause the mayo to break (not emulsify properly). If yours breaks, add a fresh egg yolk to a clean jar, start again at the bottom, and slowly drizzle in the broken mayo. It almost always comes back together.
