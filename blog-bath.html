My Go-To Reset Bath After a Long Week

By Harley  ·  Self-Care

I used to think self-care was something you either had time for or you didn't. Then I realized I was just making it too complicated. I don't need a spa day or two free hours to feel like myself again. I just need twenty minutes and a really good bath.

This is the exact routine I've been doing every week for the past year. It's simple, it costs almost nothing, and I genuinely look forward to it every single time.

What You'll Need

You don't need a lot. That's the whole point. Here's what I use every time:

My bath formula: 1–2 cups epsom salt  ·  5 drops lavender essential oil  ·  3 drops eucalyptus oil  ·  Warm (not scalding) water  ·  20 uninterrupted minutes

Why Epsom Salts?

Epsom salt is magnesium sulfate, and magnesium is something most of us are deficient in without even realizing it. Soaking in it allows your body to absorb it through your skin. I notice it most in my muscles — that heavy, achy feeling after a long week just melts away. It also helps with stress, sleep, and general inflammation. It's one of those things that sounds too simple to actually work until you try it.

The Oil Combo That Changed Everything

Lavender on its own is lovely, but when I started pairing it with eucalyptus it became something else entirely. The combination is calming and energizing at the same time — my brain gets quiet but I don't feel sluggish. I drop the oils right into the water as it's filling up so they disperse evenly. The steam carries the scent and honestly within about two minutes I can feel my shoulders drop.

The Twenty-Minute Rule

Twenty minutes is the sweet spot. Long enough to actually absorb the magnesium and let your nervous system settle, short enough that you can actually fit it into a real life. I put my phone in the other room — this is the one part I'm strict about — and I just sit. No podcast, no planning. Just the water and the smell and the quiet.

I come out of that bath feeling like a completely different person. Less reactive, less tense, more like myself. It's become my non-negotiable end-of-week ritual and I protect it like it's a meeting I can't miss — because honestly, for my mental health, it kind of is.

A Few Tips

Keep the water warm but not too hot — scalding water can actually be hard on your skin. Add the salts while the water is running so they dissolve fully. If you want to make it feel extra luxurious, light a candle. And when the twenty minutes are up, rinse off quickly with cool water to close your pores. That's it. That's the whole thing.

If you haven't tried this yet, do it this weekend. You deserve twenty minutes.

Bonus: DIY Sugar Scrub

I do this once a week, usually right before my reset bath. It takes two minutes and your skin feels completely different afterward — soft in a way that lotion alone never gets. I make a small batch, use it in the shower, and my skin is ready to actually absorb the bath soak.

Lavender Sugar Scrub Recipe:

• ½ cup white sugar
• 3 tablespoons coconut oil (melted)
• 5 drops lavender essential oil
• Optional: a few drops of vitamin E oil

Mix everything together in a small jar. Scoop out a small amount and scrub in gentle circles, then rinse. Use within 2–3 weeks.

It smells amazing, it costs almost nothing to make, and it works better than anything I've bought in a store. Start your bath night with this and the whole routine feels like an actual spa experience — from your own bathroom.

Bonus: Detox Soak Salt Recipe

This one is a step up from a basic epsom soak. I started making it when I wanted something that actually felt like it was doing something — not just relaxing, but really resetting. The bentonite clay is the difference-maker. I keep a jar of this mixed and ready to go so I never have to think about it.

Detox Soak Salt Recipe:

• 1 cup epsom salt
• ½ cup dead sea salt
• ¼ cup baking soda
• 2 tablespoons bentonite clay
• 10 drops lavender essential oil
• 5 drops eucalyptus essential oil
• 1 teaspoon vitamin E oil

Mix the dry ingredients first, then stir in the oils. Store in a glass jar with a lid. Use 1 cup per bath, added to running water. Soak for at least 20 minutes.

The dead sea salt is packed with minerals that your skin actually absorbs. The bentonite clay binds to impurities and pulls them out while you soak. The baking soda neutralizes chlorine in tap water and leaves your skin incredibly soft. The vitamin E seals everything in. I come out of this bath feeling genuinely clean in a way that regular bathing just doesn't get to.
