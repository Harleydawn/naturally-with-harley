5 Ways I Use Lavender Oil Every Single Day

By Harley  ·  Natural Living

If I had to pick just one essential oil to keep forever, it would be lavender — no contest. I know that sounds dramatic, but once you start using it the way I do, you'll get it. It's not just a pretty smell. It actually does things. Real things. And I use it every single day without even thinking about it anymore.

Here are the five ways lavender has worked its way into my daily routine — and why I genuinely can't imagine my life without it.

1. My Pillow Spray

This was the first thing I ever tried with lavender and honestly it's what got me hooked. I mix a few drops with water in a little spray bottle and spritz my pillow before bed every night. The difference in how quickly I fall asleep is real. My brain just slows down. I've had people over who asked what my bedroom smelled like and every single time it leads to a conversation about essential oils.

My recipe: 10 drops of lavender in a 2oz spray bottle filled with distilled water. Shake before each use.

2. My Morning Diffuser Blend

I run a diffuser every morning while I'm getting the kids ready. My go-to blend is lavender with a little eucalyptus. It makes the whole house smell fresh and calm at the same time — which is exactly what I need before the morning rush. I've found it helps me start the day feeling collected instead of frantic.

3. On My Son After Baths

A drop of lavender in his lotion before bed has become part of our nighttime routine. It signals to him that it's time to wind down, and honestly it works. Bedtime used to be a whole thing at our house. Now he's out faster and I think the lavender is a big part of that. It's calming in a way that doesn't feel forced.

Important: Always dilute essential oils before putting them on kids' skin. I add 1 drop to a good amount of unscented lotion — never apply oils directly to children undiluted.

4. On Bug Bites and Minor Irritations

Lavender is one of the few oils that's gentle enough to apply directly to skin in small amounts, and I use it constantly on little bug bites, minor skin irritations, and scrapes. My son knows to come find me and the lavender bottle when something stings. It soothes almost immediately and I feel so much better using this than a bunch of mystery ingredients from a tube.

5. In My Bath

At the end of a long week, a few drops of lavender in a warm bath is the reset I need. I pair it with epsom salts and just let everything go for twenty minutes. My body relaxes, my mind quiets down, and I come out actually feeling like myself again. It's become something I genuinely look forward to — and I think everyone deserves that.

Lavender is one of those things where once you start, you just keep finding new uses for it. These five have become automatic for me, but I'm always experimenting. If you haven't tried it yet, start with the pillow spray. It's simple, it's cheap, and it genuinely works.

Bonus: My DIY Bug Repellent Spray

This one deserves its own section because it has completely replaced the chemical-filled bug sprays we used to use. I started making this for my son last summer and I will never go back to the store-bought stuff. It smells amazing, it actually works, and I feel good putting it on his skin.

DIY Bug Repellent Recipe:
• 2 oz distilled water
• 1 oz witch hazel
• 10 drops lavender essential oil
• 8 drops lemon eucalyptus essential oil
• 6 drops cedarwood essential oil
• 4 drops lemongrass essential oil

Combine everything in a small spray bottle, shake well before each use, and spray on exposed skin and clothing. Reapply every 2 hours outdoors.

Lavender on its own does repel bugs — it's actually one of the reasons I started using it outside. But combined with lemon eucalyptus and cedarwood, it becomes genuinely effective. The lemon eucalyptus is the star here — it's one of the only plant-based ingredients the CDC recognizes as an effective repellent. The cedarwood adds an earthy base note and is especially good at keeping mosquitoes away.

I keep a bottle in my bag all summer. It smells great, works well, and I feel good about what's actually going on his skin. That's everything I could ask for.

Bonus: DIY Chest Rub

Cold and congestion season is when lavender really earns its place. This chest rub takes five minutes to make and I reach for it every single time my son gets stuffed up. No petroleum, no synthetic fragrance — just three ingredients that actually do something.

DIY Chest Rub Recipe:

• 3 tablespoons fractionated coconut oil
• 10 drops eucalyptus essential oil
• 6 drops lavender essential oil
• 4 drops cedarwood essential oil

Stir together and store in a small glass jar. Rub a small amount onto the chest and upper back as needed. Always dilute before applying to kids' skin.

The eucalyptus opens everything up and the cedarwood adds warmth and depth. I apply it before bed and it makes a real difference overnight. One batch lasts for months and costs almost nothing to make.

Bonus: Daily Calm Roller Blend

This is the one I put on every morning before I start my day. Ten seconds and it genuinely shifts my energy. I roll it on my wrists and the back of my neck and just take one slow breath. That's it. Such a small thing, but it sets the tone for the whole morning.

Morning Calm Roller Blend:

• 10 ml roller bottle
• 5 drops lavender essential oil
• 4 drops wild orange essential oil
• 3 drops cedarwood essential oil
• Fill the rest with fractionated coconut oil

Apply to wrists, behind ears, or back of neck. Shake before each use.

Lavender keeps you grounded, wild orange lifts your mood, and cedarwood steadies everything out. It smells incredible and I get asked about it constantly. Make a few bottles — you'll want to give them away.
