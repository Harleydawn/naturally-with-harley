Homemade Cinnamon Rolls

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Weekend mornings were made for these. Thick, gooey, smothered in cream cheese icing — and your whole house smells like a bakery while they bake. I make these on Sunday mornings and my family comes running to the kitchen every time.

Yes, they take a little time. Yes, they are absolutely worth it. I promise you will never buy store-bought again.

What You Need — Makes 12 rolls

  • Dough:
  • 1 cup warm milk
  • 2¼ tsp active dry yeast
  • ⅓ cup sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • ⅓ cup butter, softened
  • 4 cups all-purpose flour
  • Filling:
  • ⅓ cup butter, softened
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp cinnamon
  • Cream Cheese Icing:
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1½ cups powdered sugar
  • ¼ cup butter, softened
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 2–3 tbsp milk

How to Make It

  1. Dissolve yeast in warm milk with a pinch of sugar. Let sit 5 minutes until foamy.
  2. Add sugar, salt, eggs, and softened butter. Mix in flour until a soft dough forms.
  3. Knead 6–8 minutes until smooth. Cover and let rise 1 hour until doubled.
  4. Roll the dough into a large rectangle, about 15x10 inches.
  5. Spread softened butter evenly over the dough. Mix the brown sugar and cinnamon and sprinkle evenly over the butter.
  6. Roll the dough tightly from the long side into a log. Cut into 12 even rolls.
  7. Place rolls in a greased 9x13 pan. Cover and let rise 30 minutes.
  8. Preheat oven to 350°F. Bake 22–25 minutes until golden.
  9. While they bake, beat the cream cheese icing ingredients together until smooth and spreadable.
  10. Frost while the rolls are still warm so the icing melts in.

Harley's tip: Use unflavored dental floss to cut the rolls — it slides through the dough cleanly without squishing them flat. Slide the floss under the log, cross it on top, and pull. Perfect rounds every time.
