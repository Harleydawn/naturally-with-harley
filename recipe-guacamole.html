Homemade Guacamole

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Fresh guacamole takes five minutes and there is no comparison to anything you buy at a store. The stuff in those little tubs has been sitting there for who knows how long — fresh guac made with ripe avocados and real lime is a completely different thing. My kids eat it straight out of the bowl before it even makes it to the chips.

The key is ripe avocados and not over-mashing. Chunky is better.

What You Need — Makes about 1½ cups

  • 3 ripe avocados
  • Juice of 1–2 limes
  • ½ tsp salt (more to taste)
  • ¼ red onion, finely diced
  • 1–2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced (optional)
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 roma tomato, seeded and diced

How to Make It

  1. Cut the avocados in half, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
  2. Add the lime juice and salt first, then mash with a fork to your preferred texture. Chunky has more character than smooth.
  3. Fold in the red onion, garlic, jalapeño, cilantro, and tomato.
  4. Taste and adjust — more lime for brightness, more salt to bring out the flavor, more jalapeño for heat.
  5. Serve immediately with chips, tacos, eggs, or anything that holds still long enough.

Harley's tip: To keep guacamole from browning, press plastic wrap directly onto the surface before refrigerating — no air gap. The lime juice also slows oxidation. It won't last more than a day but honestly it never lasts that long anyway.
