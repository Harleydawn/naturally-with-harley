From-Scratch Brownies

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

I threw out every box mix the day I made these. They are fudgy, rich, deeply chocolatey, and made with ingredients I can actually pronounce. Eight ingredients, one bowl, one pan. The hardest part is waiting for them to cool before you cut into them — and honestly, I don't always manage that.

These are the brownies people ask me about. Every single time I bring them somewhere, someone wants the recipe.

What You Need — Makes 16 brownies (8x8 pan)

  • ½ cup (1 stick) butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • ⅓ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp baking powder

How to Make It

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease an 8x8 inch baking pan.
  2. Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over low heat. Remove from the heat and let it cool for a couple of minutes.
  3. Stir the sugar into the melted butter until combined.
  4. Add the eggs and vanilla and stir vigorously until the mixture is smooth and glossy.
  5. Add the cocoa powder, flour, salt, and baking powder. Stir until just combined — don't overmix.
  6. Pour into the prepared pan and spread evenly.
  7. Bake 25–30 minutes. A toothpick inserted in the center should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter.
  8. Cool completely in the pan before cutting. I know it's hard. Do it anyway — the texture is so much better.

Harley's tip: For cleaner slices, run your knife under hot water, wipe it dry, and cut. Repeat between each slice. Makes a huge difference in how they look when you serve them.

Want to make them extra fudgy? Add a handful of chocolate chips to the batter before baking. They melt in and create little pockets of chocolate throughout that take these completely over the top.
