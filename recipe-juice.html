Homemade Fresh Juice

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Store-bought juice is basically sugar water with a fruit label on it. Once I started making juice at home I could not go back. Real fruit, no added sugar, no concentrate — and the whole family actually drinks it without me having to ask twice. I make a big pitcher a few times a week and it never lasts long.

This is my go-to blend — orange and apple with a little fresh ginger if I'm feeling it. You can swap out the fruit for whatever you have, but this combination is the one we keep coming back to.

What You Need

  • 6 navel oranges, peeled
  • 3 medium apples, cored
  • 1 inch fresh ginger (optional but so good)
  • Pinch of salt

How to Make It

With a Juicer:

  1. Peel the oranges and core the apples. No need to peel the apples.
  2. Run everything through your juicer — oranges, apples, and ginger.
  3. Add a tiny pinch of salt and stir. It brings out the sweetness.
  4. Pour over ice and serve, or store in a sealed jar in the fridge for up to 3 days.

Without a Juicer (Blender Method):

  1. Peel the oranges, removing as much white pith as you can. Core and roughly chop the apples.
  2. Blend everything on high with ½ cup of water until completely smooth.
  3. Pour through a fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth into a pitcher, pressing to get all the juice out.
  4. Add a pinch of salt, stir, serve over ice.

Harley's tip: Room temperature fruit gives you way more juice than cold fruit straight from the fridge. Let your oranges sit out for 30 minutes before juicing — you'll notice the difference.

You can mix and match fruit endlessly once you get the hang of it. Pineapple and mango is our summer favorite. Watermelon on its own is incredible. Beet with apple and ginger is what I make when I want something that feels really nourishing. Start with the orange-apple and go from there.
