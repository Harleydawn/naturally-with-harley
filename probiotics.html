What I Wish I Knew Before Starting

Probiotics aren't all the same — and how you take them matters just as much as which one you pick.

📅

Be Consistent

The benefits build over time. Taking a probiotic once in a while does almost nothing. Daily, same time each day, for at least 4–6 weeks before you judge the results.

🍽️

Take With Food

Most probiotics survive better when taken with a meal. Food buffers stomach acid and gives the bacteria a better chance of reaching your gut alive and active.

🔬

Strains Matter

Not all probiotics do the same thing. Look at the specific strains listed, not just the CFU count. Different strains are studied for different outcomes — gut, immunity, mood.

🥗

Feed Your Gut Too

Probiotics work best alongside prebiotic foods — garlic, bananas, oats, onion. The good bacteria need something to feed on. Give them fiber and they thrive.
