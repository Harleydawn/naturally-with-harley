Garlic Herb Butter

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Compound butter sounds fancy but it is literally just softened butter mixed with garlic and herbs. I use it on everything — garlic bread, steak, grilled corn, baked potatoes, pasta, rolls straight out of the oven. It takes five minutes to make and completely transforms whatever it touches.

Make a log, wrap it in plastic wrap, and keep it in the fridge or freezer. Slice off a piece whenever you need it.

What You Need

  • 1 stick (½ cup) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced or pressed
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh chives, finely chopped
  • ½ tsp salt (or to taste)
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1 tsp lemon zest (optional — so good)

How to Make It

  1. Make sure your butter is fully softened at room temperature — it should be easy to mash with a fork.
  2. Add all ingredients to the butter and mix well with a fork until everything is evenly distributed.
  3. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.
  4. Spoon the butter onto a piece of plastic wrap and shape it into a log. Roll it up tightly and twist the ends.
  5. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour to firm up before using. Keeps in the fridge for 2 weeks, or freeze for up to 3 months.

Harley's tip: To make garlic bread in a hurry — slice a baguette, spread this butter on each piece, and broil for 2–3 minutes until golden and bubbling. It's the best garlic bread you will ever eat and it takes under 5 minutes total.
