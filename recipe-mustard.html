Homemade Yellow Mustard

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Homemade yellow mustard takes about five minutes to make and tastes exactly like the classic — bright, tangy, and that signature bold yellow color. The main ingredient is ground mustard seed, which is incredibly cheap and easy to find. Once you realize how simple it is, you'll start wondering why you ever bought the bottle.

It needs to rest overnight before serving. That wait is what mellows the sharp raw bite and brings out the flavor.

What You Need — Makes about ¾ cup

  • ¼ cup yellow mustard seeds (or ¼ cup dry ground mustard powder)
  • ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
  • ¼ cup water
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp turmeric (for color)
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp honey (optional, for a slight sweetness)

How to Make It

  1. If using whole mustard seeds, grind them in a spice grinder or blender until they're a coarse powder. If using ground mustard powder, skip this step.
  2. Combine the mustard with the vinegar, water, salt, turmeric, and garlic powder in a small saucepan.
  3. Whisk until smooth, then place over medium-low heat.
  4. Cook, stirring constantly, for about 5–7 minutes until the mustard thickens to your preferred consistency.
  5. Stir in honey if using. Remove from heat and let cool.
  6. Transfer to a jar and refrigerate overnight before using. It'll be sharp right away but smooths out beautifully by morning. Keeps for up to 2 months.

Harley's tip: If the mustard is too thick after refrigerating, stir in a teaspoon of cold water to loosen it. If it's too thin, it'll continue to firm up over the next day or two in the fridge. Fresh mustard is a little sharper than store-bought — that's normal and it's good.
