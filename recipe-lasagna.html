Homemade Lasagna

By Harley  ·  From My Kitchen

Homemade lasagna is a project — I won't lie. But it feeds a crowd, it freezes beautifully, and there is nothing on earth more satisfying to pull out of the oven. The layers of meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted mozzarella make it worth every single step.

I make a big batch of my homemade pasta sauce first and let it simmer low and slow. That sauce is what makes this lasagna taste like it took all day — because the sauce part did.

What You Need — Serves 8–10

  • 1 lb lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef (or half beef, half Italian sausage)
  • 3 cups homemade or store-bought tomato pasta sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley or basil to finish

How to Make It

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Cook lasagna noodles according to package directions until just al dente. Drain and lay flat on oiled parchment paper so they don't stick together.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks. Drain excess fat. Add the pasta sauce and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. In a bowl, mix together the ricotta, egg, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  4. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13 baking dish.
  5. Layer: noodles → ricotta mixture → meat sauce → mozzarella. Repeat layers, finishing with meat sauce and mozzarella on top. Sprinkle Parmesan over everything.
  6. Cover tightly with foil and bake 45 minutes. Remove foil and bake 15 more minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
  7. Let rest 15 minutes before cutting. This step matters — it firms up and slices cleanly.

Harley's tip: This freezes perfectly. I always make two and freeze one unbaked. When ready to use, thaw overnight in the fridge and bake as directed, adding about 15 extra minutes covered. It tastes just as good as fresh.
